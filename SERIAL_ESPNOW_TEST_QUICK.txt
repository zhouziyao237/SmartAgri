SmartAgri V2.1.8b SERIAL + ESP-NOW QUICK TEST

1) Flash C3 and ESP32.
2) Open each board's onboard USB COM at 115200 8N1, CRLF.
3) Send: AT, AT+GMR, AT+PINS?, AT+STATUS?, AT+MAC?
4) Configure Classic ESP32 as MASTER device 1, network/group 1, channel 6, TESTMODE=1.
5) Record MASTER AT+MAC?.
6) Configure C3 as SLAVE device 1001 with AT+GATEWAY=MASTER_MAC, same network/group/channel, TESTMODE=1.
7) MASTER should see +LINKRX:HELLO,SRC=1001 about every 5s.
8) C3: AT+TESTSEND="C3_TO_ESP32"; MASTER should see +TESTRX.
9) MASTER: AT+TESTSEND="ESP32_TO_C3"; C3 should see +TESTRX.
10) Before STM32 integration set TESTMODE=0, SAVE/APPLY.

C3 STM32 business pins are GPIO7 TX / GPIO6 RX @921600. GPIO21/20 stay UART0 Debug/AT.
