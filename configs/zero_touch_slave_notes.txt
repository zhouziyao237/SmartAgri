SmartAgri V2.4.8 zero-touch SLAVE deployment

Normal deployment no longer requires AT+GATEWAY.
A configured SLAVE with GATEWAY=CLEAR, AUTODISCOVERY=1, CHANNELSCAN=1 will:
1. Broadcast LINK_HELLO to FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF.
2. Scan Wi-Fi channels 1..13 until it receives LINK_ACK.
3. Learn and persist MASTER MAC + actual channel.
4. Use unicast for normal traffic after binding.

A factory UNCONFIGURED V2.4.8 ESP also starts ESP-NOW with a deterministic provisional Device ID.
A V2.4.8 MASTER advertises that provisional node to the PC so Web can remotely set Role/Device/Network/Group.
AT remains available for service/recovery only.
